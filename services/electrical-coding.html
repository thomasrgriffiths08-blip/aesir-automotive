Electrical
& Coding

Modern BMWs are computers with wheels. Electrical faults get traced properly — batteries registered, modules coded, warning lights out for good.

Random warning lights, batteries that drain overnight, windows and modules with minds of their own — electrical faults are where pattern knowledge earns its keep. We trace the circuit rather than swapping parts on hope.

New battery? On a modern BMW it must be registered to the car or charging goes wrong and the electronics misbehave — we do it properly. Replacement modules get coded to the vehicle so everything works as BMW intended.

An electrical fault another garage couldn't pin down is a normal week here. Bring the history and we'll bring the wiring diagrams.
