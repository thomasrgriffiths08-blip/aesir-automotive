MOT Prep
& Repairs

A pre-MOT check costs less than a retest and a lot less than a fine. We go through the test points first, fix what needs fixing, and remind you automatically next year.

We run the car through the MOT checklist before the tester does: lights, tyres, brakes, suspension play, corrosion, emissions readiness. Anything that would fail gets flagged with an honest quote — anything borderline gets flagged as an advisory before it becomes a failure.

Then the clever bit: our reminder system texts you four weeks before your next MOT is due, again a week out, and the day before your slot. Set it once, never miss a date again.

Repairs from a failed test elsewhere are welcome too — bring the failure sheet and we'll price the fixes straight.
