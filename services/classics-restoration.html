Classics &
Restoration

The Instagram bio says it in three words: late 20th century classics. This is the work the workshop exists for.

M10 heads off E21s, M20 straight-sixes out of E30s, an S50B32 rebuilt and refitted to a Z3 M Coupé — the classic BMW work here isn't a sideline, it's the reason the business exists. The profile picture is a shark-nose 7 Series for a reason.

Sympathetic means the car stays the car: period-correct parts where they matter, sensible modern substitutes where they're genuinely better, and honesty about which is which. Mechanical restoration, recommissioning after storage, and the patient fault-finding old cars demand.

If you're buying a classic BMW, have us inspect it first — we know where these cars rust, wear and get bodged.
