Pre-Purchase
Inspections

We inspect the car like we'd have to fix it afterwards — because if you buy it, we probably will. Full written report before you hand over money.

A used BMW can hide expensive news behind a clean valet. We put the car on the ramp and go through it the way a mechanic does when he knows he'll be the one fixing whatever's found: engine health, gearbox behaviour, suspension wear, corrosion, electronics, service history and the tell-tale signs of bodged past repairs.

You get a written report with photos and a straight verdict — including the negotiation ammunition of what the faults would actually cost to put right.

On M cars and classics this matters double: the difference between a good one and a money pit rarely shows on a test drive.

  • Engine & gearbox health checks
  • Corrosion & repair-history review
  • Electronics & module scan

  • Honest walk-away advice
