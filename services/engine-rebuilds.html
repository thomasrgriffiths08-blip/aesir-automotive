Service 01 · The specialty

Engine Rebuilds
& Machining

Most garages send engines away. Here they come apart and go back together on our own bench — which is why other garages send their difficult BMWs to us.

Engine work is what this workshop is known for. Big-end bearings on an E60 M5's S85 V10, skimmed and rebuilt M10 and M20 heads from classic E21s and E30s, an S50B32 stripped, rebuilt and refitted to a Z3 M Coupé that "fired straight up, and ran sweet from the get go" — all documented on the workshop feed.

Every rebuild starts with evidence: compression and leak-down figures, oil analysis where useful, and photos of what we find at every stage. You'll see the worn bearing shells before we talk about the bill — transparency is the thing our Google reviews mention most.

Because the machining and assembly happen in-house, there's no middle-man markup and no mystery about who actually did the work. One pair of hands strips it, measures it, rebuilds it and road-tests it.

At a glance
  • Bottom-end rebuilds & bearing replacement
  • Cylinder head skimming & rebuilds
  • M-division engines — S50, S54, S62, S85
  • Classic straight-six & M10 four-cylinder work
  • Compression / leak-down diagnosis first
  • Photo evidence at every stage
