Service 06 · Cold again

Air
Conditioning

A quiet loss of cold air usually means a slow leak, not just low gas. We find it, fix it, and re-gas to specification.

An air-con system that needs a re-gas every summer has a leak — re-gassing it without finding the leak is throwing money into the atmosphere. We leak-test first, repair what's letting go, then charge the system to BMW's specification.

While we're in there, cabin filters get checked and changed — the cheap job that transforms how the car smells and how hard the fan works.

Book it before the first hot week, when everyone else remembers at once.
