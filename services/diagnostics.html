Diagnostics

An engine light tells you almost nothing. Dealer-level diagnostics kit and twenty-plus years of BMW pattern knowledge tell you what's actually wrong.

Code readers guess; diagnosis proves. We use dealer-level equipment to read live data, freeze frames and module states across the whole car — then test the suspect component properly before condemning it. You see the evidence before any work is agreed.

It's why customers arrive here after other garages have given up. One reviewer was "recommended to Neil by another garage that couldn't economically fix my BMW 320i." A Ferrari F430 came in with multiple misfires that had defeated everyone else — the feed has the pictures.

If the fix is worth doing we'll quote it straight; if it isn't, we'll tell you that too. Either way you leave knowing what's real.
