02What we collect

Only what we need to book your car in and do the work.

  • When you book online: your name, your mobile number, and — if you choose to give them, both fields are optional — your vehicle registration and model. Plus the service, date and time you picked.
  • When you call or message us on WhatsApp: whatever you tell us, which is usually your name, number and what the car is doing.
  • When we work on your car: the vehicle details and a record of the work carried out, which we keep as the service history.

We do not ask for payment card details through this website. Nothing is paid online — you pay at the workshop, after the work.

03Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To hold your booking and prepare for the job — lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing that contract.
  • To contact you about your car — to confirm, to reschedule, to tell you what we found, or to let you know it's ready. Same basis as above.
  • To keep a record of work carried out — legitimate interests: a garage needs a service record to stand behind its work and to advise you correctly next time. Some records are also kept to meet tax and accounting duties.

We do not use your details for marketing, we do not send newsletters, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

04Who we share it with

  • Service providers who host this website and its booking diary. They store the data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Suppliers and specialists, where a job requires it — for example a machine shop, or a parts supplier who needs the vehicle identification number to supply the correct part.
  • Our accountant, and HMRC or other authorities where the law requires it.

05How long we keep it

Booking details are removed from the online diary once the job is done and the record has been written up.

Records of work carried out on a vehicle are kept for as long as they are useful to you as a service history and to us in standing behind our work, and financial records are kept for the period required by tax law — currently six years.

If you would like your details removed sooner, see section 08.

06Where it's stored

The website and its booking diary are hosted by third-party providers. Some providers may store or process data outside the UK; where that happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers have in place for international transfers.

If you would like to know exactly which providers are involved at any given time, ask and we'll tell you.

07Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no analytics or advertising pixel on it.

It does use your browser's local storage for two strictly functional things: remembering the current state of the booking diary so the page still works if your connection drops, and remembering that the introduction animation has already played so you don't sit through it twice. These are necessary for features you asked for, and they stay on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Two third-party services are loaded by the site and may set their own cookies or receive your IP address: Google Fonts (typefaces) and, on the contact page, an embedded Google Map. If you would rather not load the map, simply don't open the contact page — the address is also written in plain text in the footer of every page.

08Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it's wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete it, where we don't have a legal reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it.
  • Transfer it to you or another provider in a portable format.

Just call or message the workshop. We won't charge you, and we'll respond within one month.

09Complaints

If something's gone wrong with your information, please tell us first — it's usually quickest to fix directly.

You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
