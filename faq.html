Booking & visiting
How do I book my car in?
Online any time via the booking page — pick a service, a day and a real open slot, and you're confirmed with a reference in under a minute. Or call / WhatsApp Neil on 07956 658177.
Do I pay anything online?
No. Booking online costs nothing and there's no deposit — you pay for the work, after the work, once you've seen what was done.
Can I change or cancel a booking?
Yes, free — reply to your confirmation, or call/WhatsApp and we'll move it.

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 8am–6pm. Weekends by arrangement — ask.
Prices & parts
How does pricing work?
You get the price before the work, with evidence — photos of what we found and a straight explanation. Reviewers call the pricing fair; nobody gets surprise invoices here.
Do you use genuine parts?
Genuine or OEM-quality, matched to the job and the car. On classics we're honest about which period-correct parts matter and which modern substitutes are genuinely better.
Will servicing here affect my BMW warranty?
No — servicing to the manufacturer schedule with approved-quality parts keeps your warranty intact, and we update the digital service history the same way a dealer does.
The work
Do you only work on BMWs?
The BMW group is the specialty — BMW, M cars, MINI, Rolls-Royce — plus late-20th-century classics. The odd Ferrari has been on the ramps too. See cars we work on.
Do you really rebuild engines in-house?
Yes — on the bench, in the workshop. Bearings, skimmed heads, complete bottom ends: M V10s to classic straight-sixes. It's the reason other garages send difficult cars here.
My engine light is on — what should I do?
Don't guess and don't panic-buy parts. Book diagnostics: we read the actual fault data with dealer-level kit and show you the evidence before anything is agreed.
Can you look at a car I'm thinking of buying?
Yes — pre-purchase inspections are a specialty, especially on M cars and classics where the difference between a good one and a money pit doesn't show on a test drive. Full written report with photos.
How long do jobs take?
A service is usually same-day. Diagnostics depends on the fault — we'll tell you honestly once we've seen the data. Engine rebuilds are booked as projects with a clear timeline up front.
Who actually works on my car?
Neil. Only Neil. The person you speak to on the phone is the person under the bonnet — that's the whole point of the place.
