The workshop

One pair of hands.

Æsir Automotive is run by Neil Haddow from Pond Farm, on the green edge of Uxbridge. No service desk, no upsell scripts — the person you speak to is the person under your bonnet.

The passion here is late-20th-century classics and proper engineering: engines rebuilt on the bench, heads machined, faults traced to the wire. It's why other garages send their difficult BMWs this way — and why Ferraris and a Rolls-Royce Ghost have found their way onto the ramps.
