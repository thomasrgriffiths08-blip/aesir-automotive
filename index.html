Æsir Automotive is run by Neil Haddow from Pond Farm, on the green edge of Uxbridge. No service desk, no upsell scripts — the person you speak to is the person under your bonnet.

The passion here is late-20th-century classics and proper engineering: engines rebuilt on the bench, heads machined, faults traced to the wire. It's why other garages send their difficult BMWs this way — and why Ferraris and a Rolls-Royce Ghost have found their way onto the ramps.

Asked
§ 06Straight answersMore on the FAQ page

Asked all the time.

Do you only work on BMWs?
We specialise in the BMW group — BMW, M cars, MINI and Rolls-Royce — plus late-20th-century BMW classics. The occasional Ferrari has been on the ramps too, from an F430 misfire diagnosis to classic work.
Do you do full engine rebuilds?
Yes — in-house, on the bench. Bearings, skimmed heads and complete bottom-end rebuilds, from BMW M V10s to classic straight-sixes. Engine work is the workshop's specialty.
Where is the workshop?
3d Pond Farm, Newyears Green Lane, Uxbridge UB9 6LX — between Harefield, Ickenham and Ruislip on the north-west edge of London.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Outside those hours you can book online 24/7 or message the workshop on WhatsApp at 07956 658177.
How do I book my car in?
Book online any time in the booking section, call or WhatsApp Neil on 07956 658177. Online bookings get an instant on-screen confirmation with a reference, and the slot is held in the workshop diary straight away.
Who runs Æsir Automotive?
Neil Haddow, director since 2020. It's a one-mechanic workshop — the person you speak to is the person working on your car. Reviewers on Google describe the work as professional, fairly priced and completed to a high standard.
