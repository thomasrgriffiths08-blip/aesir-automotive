The workshop

One pair of hands.
Every job.

Æsir Automotive is what a garage looks like when one obsessive builds it around doing the work properly — not around a service desk.

Æsir Automotive Ltd was incorporated on 30 June 2020, but the knowledge behind it goes back decades. It's run by Neil Haddow — director, master technician, and the only person who will ever touch your car. No service advisors, no flat-rate juniors, no upsell scripts.

The name comes from the Æsir — the Norse gods of strength and craft — and the workshop lives up to it with the kind of work most garages outsource: complete engine rebuilds on the bench, cylinder heads machined, bottom ends measured and rebuilt by hand.

The Instagram bio reads "late 20th century classics" and that's the house passion: E21s, E30s, Z3 Ms and shark-nosed 7 Series. But the ramps have held everything from a Rolls-Royce Ghost (oil leaks and a Spirit of Ecstasy repair) to a Ferrari F430 with misfires two other garages couldn't crack.

That's the pattern reviewers keep describing: other garages send their difficult cars here. One customer put it simply — "I was recommended to Neil by another garage that couldn't economically fix my BMW 320i."

If you want the showroom experience, the dealer will happily take your money. If you want the person answering the phone to be the person holding the torque wrench, that's this place.
