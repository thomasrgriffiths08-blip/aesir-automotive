How it works.

STEP 01Call rings out

The system watches the workshop line. If a call isn't answered within the ring window, it's logged as missed with the caller's number.

STEP 02Text fires back

Within seconds, the caller gets a personal text — not a robot voice, not voicemail. The reply window is open before they've tried the next garage.

STEP 03Neil takes over

The conversation lands in the workshop inbox. Neil replies between jobs, offers a slot, and the booking goes straight into the live diary.
